No pictures are bundled in this build.

Seven pages were read (wallhaven `1qo1y1`, `9okw2k`, `e8v198`, `zp527w`, `g76roe`, `pow5ep`, `4ox7l9`).
They are game or anime fan art, and none says redistribution is allowed. Further pages returned
HTTP 429, so the rest were not shipped either. The Eris Greyrat (Mushoku Tensei) files were
excluded without a page check, as requested.

When a picture with a clear license is added to this folder, named `SkyRose - <title>.<ext>`,
first launch copies it into `~/Pictures/Wallpapers/Static/` (never overwriting, skipping a
duplicate hash) and the uninstaller removes only those copies.
